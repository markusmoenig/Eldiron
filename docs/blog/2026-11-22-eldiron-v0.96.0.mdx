Eldiron v0.96.0 brings **2D Iso** to Creator and expands the shared RPG rules
with gathering, crafting, and learning. It also integrates **MaterialZ**,
a tool for creating textures and 3D models from editable recipes. You can
see all of this in **Wayfarer’s Rest**, a playable village you can open and
edit in Creator.

## A Day in Wayfarer’s Rest

I wanted an example that shows what happens after you’ve placed the buildings
and the characters. Start at the inn in the morning, talk to Rowan, then visit
Mara’s tannery. Give her time to tan a hide and stitch a pair of boots, and you
can buy what she made. Across the stone bridge, deer stay in Deer Wood.
When evening comes, villagers go out to light their lamps, Elin lights the
North Gate bonfire, and the village settles down for the night. Beyond the
gate is Blackstone Crypt.

Those routines are built from **Behavior Nodes**. A character can walk to a
placed item and perform a named intent, such as lighting a lantern or using a
bed. In the editor, click a villager or an item with the **Entity tool** to
read its schedule and interactions in **Behavior Overview**.

Open the village through **File → New → Browse Examples → Wayfarer’s Rest**.
The [village walkthrough](/docs/creator/wayfarers_rest) takes you from the inn
to the tannery and on to the gate.

## Build and Play in 2D Iso

The new **2D Iso** project template gives you an isometric grid, directional
characters, and a playable starter map. Bring in a PNG sheet or separate
sprites, set their ground-cell size and pivot, and group named directions and
animation frames on import. **Kenney Isometric Dungeon** is a second
playable example built with imported bitmap artwork.

You can mix imported sprites and MaterialZ artwork in the same map. Paint
grass and details, turn editable lines into terrain paths, and shape hills
and plateaus. You can also drag a building’s sector footprint with its
attached content in one undoable move.

Creator and the graphical player now draw the scene and interface on the GPU.
In 2D Iso, reusable terrain chunks and streamed tile bakes reduce repeated
work while sunlight and local lights continue to update as you edit and play.
See the [isometric tileset guide](/docs/building_maps/isometric_sprite_tilesets)
for the import and placement workflow.

## MaterialZ in Creator

A MaterialZ recipe is a set of instructions that Creator turns into artwork.
For the masonry in the screenshot, a grid lays out the bricks, noise varies
their appearance, and a color ramp gives the stone its shades. Change the
recipe and preview the result in the editor. You can use the material on
a 2D tile or a 3D wall. Rect compositions layer reusable surfaces for paths,
verges, and transitions.

Mesh recipes build the shapes of objects such as walls and trees. They can
also define dimensions, connectors, and walkable footprints. In 2D Iso,
Creator renders those models as sprites. In the Appearance workspace you can
inspect character and item models, turn them around, and preview their
animations. Villagers can have different builds, faces, hairstyles, and outfits
while using the shared Human model. Farm animals and woodland wildlife use
their own shared species models.
Read more in the [MaterialZ tutorials](/docs/building_maps/materialz/start-here) and
the [Appearance guide](/docs/characters_items/appearance).

## One Ruleset for Classic and Sandbox RPGs

I want Eldiron to ship with a complete RPG ruleset that creators can pick
from. A classic adventure might offer Warrior and Ranger, level progression,
and a few carefully chosen weapons and spells. A sandbox can start with a
flexible character and let players learn skills and take up trades as they play.
A ranger can learn to work leather, and a leatherworker can go adventuring.
Both styles draw from the same rules, and a single game can combine them.

In Creator, choose the classes that fit your game and drag the characters
and items you want from the project tree onto the map. The bundled ruleset
supplies their gameplay rules. Your world determines what players can find
and learn through the resources, equipment, and teachers you place there.

Wayfarer’s Rest shows how that can work. Trees supply wood and resin, rivers
and wells fill vessels, and skeleton and animal remains supply bone and hide.

The **Crafting & Knowledge** journal shows the materials, tools, stations,
and lessons a recipe needs. Bone and leather armor have full sets you can
make and repair. Compatible equipment can take more than one enchantment,
including inscriptions of the Bear and of the Fox.
See the [Official Rules](/docs/official_rules) for the production chains.

## A First Look at Dedicated Multiplayer

Dedicated multiplayer already works in v0.96. A server runs the authored world
and saves character progress and world state between sessions. Players can
connect from desktop and WebGPU browser clients, and Creator can export a
client package for your own server. See the
[Public Client Export guide](/docs/creator/public_client_export) for the setup.

We’re preparing a public multiplayer demo so you can join a shared world
and explore it with other players soon.
