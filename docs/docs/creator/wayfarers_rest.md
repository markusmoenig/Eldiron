# Wayfarer’s Rest

Wayfarer’s Rest is the village in `Village2DIso.eldiron`. In Creator, choose
**File → New → Browse Examples → Wayfarer’s Rest**. Select the **Game tool**
(`K`), then press **Play**. Walk with `WASD` or the arrow keys, or click the
ground. Press `I` for **Talk**, `U` for **Use**, or `O` for **Open/Close**,
then click the character or item you want to interact with.

## A morning in the village

1. **Start at the inn.** For this route, set Creator’s time slider to **09:30**
   before pressing Play. Find Rowan at the inn counter and talk to him. He’ll
   tell you about the inn and the road to the North Gate.
2. **Visit Mara’s tannery.** During working hours, Mara uses bark and water to
   tan hide and turns leather and cord into boots and hoods. Give her time to
   finish her batch, then use **Talk** to see what she has for sale. Buy a piece
   if you have enough coin. Her stock is finite.
3. **Open the journal with `J`.** Choose a leather recipe to see its materials,
   tool, and station requirements. Mara can teach Advanced Leatherworking once
   you reach Leatherworking 10. Press `U` and click Mara to ask for the lesson.
4. **Cross the stone bridge into Deer Wood.** Look for deer among the trees.
   Back on the village side, visit Elin at the North Gate and ask about
   Blackstone Crypt. Set the time to **19:00** to see the villagers’ lamps and
   Elin’s bonfire as their evening routines run.

To see how the village was built, select the **Entity tool** and click Mara
or one of the lanterns. The **Behavior Overview** summarizes the character’s
routine or the item’s interactions. You can inspect entities during Play while
the editor has input. Follow the guides to
[Mara’s crafting and teaching](authoring#see-crafting-and-teaching-in-wayfarers-rest),
[Rowan’s appearance and equipment](../characters_items/entity_nodes#equipment-and-visual-appearance)
and [Elin’s bonfire routine](../characters_items/behavior_examples#light-the-north-gate-bonfire).
