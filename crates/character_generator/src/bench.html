What this bench verifies
Silhouette, joint motion, facial proportions, equipment attachment, garment coverage and pixel shading at different scales. Orc tusks and the separate-bone skeleton are initial anatomy prototypes. The WebGL inspection shader is approximate; Eldiron scene depth, terrain, lighting and gameplay still require the SceneVM scene demo. Locomotion here runs in place. All poses repeat endlessly.
